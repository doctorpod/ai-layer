# My AI commandments
I like these, they align well with the [[Permaculture#Core ethics]]

---
1. **For the common good** — I use AI only for the good of myself and others, to uphold health, happiness, truth, fundemental rights and common decency
2. **Mindful and proportional** — I use AI intentionally, not casually — I don't reach for it out of laziness or habit, because every query has a real environmental cost. I keep AI use proportionate to the task — I don't use a sledgehammer when a teaspoon will do.
3. **Own the output** — if I can't explain or stand behind it, it's not mine to publish. AI can draft, but I decide.
4. **Tool, not a crutch** — I engage with the problem myself first, then use AI to challenge, extend or accelerate my thinking.
5. **Honest about own abilities** — I regularly work without AI assistance to keep my skills sharp and know where I actually stand.
6. **Transparency** — I don't hide AI use from collaborators. If it helped me, [[citing AI help|I say so]].
7. **Share what works** — if I find an effective way to use AI, I bring it to those around me. I don't use it to get ahead at others' expense.
8. **Curious, not competitive** — I don't measure myself against others' output. I measure myself against my own standards.
9. **Review output critically** — AI is confidently wrong often enough that scepticism is always warranted. I engage with everything it gives me rather than accepting it passively.
10. **Respect consumers** — if AI generates something complex or lengthy, it's my responsibility to make it accessible and not burden others with it unreasonably.
